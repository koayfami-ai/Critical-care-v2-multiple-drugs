Noradrenaline

Dopamine

Standard dose: 1 – 3 mcg/kg/min

High dose: 3 – 20 mcg/kg/min

Adrenaline

Standard: 0.01 – 0.05 mcg/kg/min

High: > 0.05 up to 0.5 mcg/kg/min

Vasopressin

Fixed rate: 0.01 – 0.03 unit/min (usual vasoplegic shock)

Higher dose: 0.04 unit/min (RV failure, post-cardiotomy syndrome)

Standard Dilution: 1 unit/hr = 1 mL/hr (50 units in 50 mL NS)
